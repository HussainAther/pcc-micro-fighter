# Validation Roadmap

1. v0.1 — legal deterministic simulator, neutral baseline, provisional policy mechanisms, descriptive observables.
2. Freeze a balance/competitiveness protocol across independent seeds and seat/order swaps. Do not require a rock-paper-scissors cycle.
3. Implement a second independent policy family before construct recovery.
4. Test Pressure as spatial commitment -> response/space constriction -> consequence.
5. Test Control as information -> context alignment -> intervention.
6. Test Chaos with value-preserving unpredictability and exploitability/performance guards.
7. Only after those synthetic tests should the environment be added to `pcc-cross-game`.
8. A graphical/playable layer may be added later but must remain separable from the headless scientific core.


## Current status after v0.5

The first prospectively justified Family B Control intervention reduced, but did not eliminate, Pressure dominance under the unchanged competitiveness gate. Do not begin construct recovery yet. Any further revision should be justified by a new decomposition of the residual imbalance rather than direct win-rate tuning.

## Current status after v0.7

The prospectively specified sustained-threat defense/recovery intervention worsened Family B Pressure-vs-Control under the unchanged competitiveness gate. This intervention is therefore rejected as a balancing mechanism. Construct recovery remains blocked. The next revision, if any, must be motivated by a new decomposition of why deterministic retreat under close sustained Pressure sacrifices value rather than by direct win-rate tuning.
